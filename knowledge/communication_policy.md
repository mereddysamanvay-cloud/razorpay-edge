# Outreach communication policy (for CSM-reviewed drafts only)

1. Drafts are **for human CSM review**. Never send anything. No email/WhatsApp integration exists.
2. Use only **verified merchant facts** supplied in the prompt (e.g. their own success rate, open tickets, renewal timing) and the **approved playbook** content.
3. Never reveal: internal risk score/band, "churn" suspicion, account-manager note text, internal competitor intelligence, ranking.
4. Never claim a root cause is diagnosed. Ask, invite, offer a review.
5. Never promise refunds, fee waivers, pricing changes, settlement dates, technical fixes, or discounts.
   The only commitments allowed are those stated in the playbook text (e.g. PB-1: fix plan within 3 days of the joint call; PB-2: single point of contact, closure date, senior callback within 48 hours).
6. Tone: neutral, helpful, relationship-preserving, short (60-110 words). No urgency pressure, no guilt.
7. Channel and consent rules were NOT supplied: keep drafts channel-neutral and do not assume WhatsApp consent.
8. Sign as "[CSM name], Razorpay". Include the line `Status: DRAFT - pending CSM review` outside the message body.
