# Segment guidance: same playbook, different delivery

Playbooks come from the supplied P2_Playbooks sheet and are not modified. Only the *delivery style* differs.

| Segment | Delivery |
|---|---|
| Enterprise | Account Manager or senior CSM leads; executive-sponsor level conversation where the playbook calls for it; specialist team (Payments Ops, product) joins the call. Revenue exposure is large, so the cost of a miss is high; prepare facts before the call. |
| Mid | Named CSM leads; bring in specialists when needed. Concise, data-backed message. |
| Small | Light-touch: one short message or call, minimal asks on the merchant's time, no heavy workshops. Naturally low volume and low usage are expected and are not risk by themselves. |

Never offer commercial concessions, refunds, fee waivers, technical fixes or specific dates that the supplied playbooks do not state.
